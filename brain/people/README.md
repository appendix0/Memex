# people

**Goes here:** A human being. One Book per person. `type: person`.

**Does NOT go here:** A company or team (that is a `project` or a `note`). An
event someone attended (that is a dated entry on their Book's `## Timeline`).

**Every Book on this shelf is `visibility: vault`, without exception.**
[[RESOLVER]] Test 0 is not a judgment call here, and `memex/edits.py` refuses to
create a `type: person` Book without the label.

Standing guidance *about* a person is not a second person Book — it is a `note`
carrying `about:` with that person's slug. The aspect is the filename; the tie
is the facet.
