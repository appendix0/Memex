# Germination trial

**This is an example Book.** It shows the shape of a `research` Book: **timeline
only, append-only, no compiled-truth layer an agent may rewrite.** Results
accumulate in order. An agent may flag a contradiction between two of them; only
the owner resolves one.

Note what is *absent*: there is no summary paragraph above a `---` that gets
updated as understanding changes. On this shelf, that layer would let an agent
quietly reconcile two conflicting results, and the reconciliation would be
invisible afterwards because the surviving text reads as though it were always
the only result.

---

## Timeline

2026-09-09 — **Follow-up, 24 pots, controller off.** 0 of 24 reached the
germination criterion under the fixed-clock schedule alone, against 18 of 24 in
the trailing-median arm. Run `trial-0906-b`. This is the same comparison as the
2026-09-06 entry with the confound removed: the earlier arms differed in pot
position as well as in schedule.

2026-09-06 — **First trial, 48 pots, split by schedule.** Germination reached
67.3% under the trailing-hour median against 29.4% under the latest-reading
schedule. Water use did not rise with it: 71.2 L/week against 74.5 L/week. Run
`trial-0906-a`.

Those two figures together answer the objection that the controller is only a
timer: a timer that watered more would raise both numbers.

⚠️ Pot position was not randomised in this trial. The 2026-09-09 follow-up
exists because of that, and supersedes this one for the schedule comparison. The
water-use figure is unaffected.
